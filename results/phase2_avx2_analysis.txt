INSTRUMENT (what changed vs K1-K3, and why it had to)
================================================================================
- campaign logs: results/phase2_avx2_campaign_<target>.windows.log, whose own
  header records: iters=4000 budget=0.05 warmup=2 run_to_cap=1 reps=5 pin=cpu2
  maxwin=9 minwin=9. Window = min(4000 samples, 50 ms); 9 windows x 5 reps =
  45 reps per (impl,size); 5850 rep lines per target, migrated=0, cpu=2 on all.
- H5 (results/h4_diagnostics.md) showed the K1-K3 window (~0.1 ms, driver default
  ITERS=100) was short enough that per-window spread measured interference
  sampling rather than the kernel. Two superseded campaigns are kept on disk as
  evidence and are NOT used for any claim:
      results/phase2_avx2_*_shortwin_*  (ITERS=100, the K1-K3 window: 40-50% spreads)
      results/phase2_avx2_budget5s_*    (ITERS=4000 BUDGET=5 s: the 4096-sample cap
                                         binds before the timer, so every window is
                                         cap-decided and mid sizes run multi-second
                                         windows; ~8x slower, no extra information)
- These files are results/*.csv and results/*.log, i.e. gitignored by design; they
  stay on disk and the superseded ones are described here so the record is readable
  from git alone.
